# Nuclear-Management-Simulator-python-
This is a terminal based python game putting you as the manager of a nuclear reactor. Have fun!!!

This has you start off as a nuclear power plant manager. It is a pretty simple game. 

Your objective is for your reactor to hit 1000 megawatts of power before the reactor temperature hits 1000 degrees Celsius. Each turn, you will do one action. 
These are what the four turn options are comprised of:
1.  Insert fuel rods: Insert fuel rods into the reactor generating power and increasing temperature. 
2.  Pump Coolant: Pump 20% of total coolant capacity to cool the reactor by -110 C
3.  Vent reactor: Leak heat out of the reactor, draining temperature but also some of your power too!
4.  Refill coolant: If you run out of coolant you may use one of your turns to refill it, refilling it by 35% capacity. 

NOTE: Unless you pump coolant, the reactor temperature will increase by 40-80 degrees Celsius each turn, adding on to any heat you artificially create. If you try to pump coolant with none left, the turn will also generate heat and skip you. TRACK YOUR RESOURCES!!
